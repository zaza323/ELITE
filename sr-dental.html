The Dental Department at Elite Medical Care provides comprehensive oral and dental medicine and surgery, offering preventive, restorative and cosmetic care with modern technologies and equipment, delivered by specialized dental staff.

We aim to protect oral and dental health and to offer patients a comfortable and safe treatment experience, focusing on accurate diagnosis and the right treatment plan for every case to achieve the best results and a healthy, beautiful smile.

Dental Surgery Services

We provide comprehensive dental surgery services, including jaw surgery, dental implants, orthodontics, pediatric dental care, and nerve and gum surgery, ensuring optimal oral health outcomes.
